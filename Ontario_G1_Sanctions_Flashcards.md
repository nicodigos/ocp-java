# Ontario G1 Sanctions Flashcard Bank

Based on the *Official Ministry of Transportation (MTO) Driver's Handbook*, especially “The demerit point system,” “Other ways to lose your licence,” and “Suspensions.” This is a study aid, not legal advice; current legislation controls.

**How to study:** Read the offence or situation on the front, state the specific sanction, and then reveal the answer.

## Sanctions deck

| # | Topic | Question | Answer |
|---:|---|---|---|
| S001 | Novice demerit points | **What sanction applies when a novice driver accumulates two or more demerit points?** | A warning letter. |
| S002 | Novice demerit points | **What sanction applies when a novice driver reaches six demerit points?** | A second warning letter encouraging improved driving behaviour. |
| S003 | Novice demerit points | **What sanction applies when a novice driver accumulates nine or more demerit points?** | A 60-day licence suspension from the date the licence is surrendered. |
| S004 | Novice demerit points | **What can happen if a novice driver does not surrender a licence after reaching nine or more points?** | The driver can lose the licence for up to two years. |
| S005 | Novice demerit points | **After a novice driver's 60-day demerit-point suspension, to how many points is the record reduced?** | Four points. |
| S006 | Novice demerit points | **What sanction may apply if a novice driver reaches nine demerit points again after a suspension?** | A six-month licence suspension. |
| S007 | Fully licensed demerit points | **What sanction applies when a fully licensed driver reaches six demerit points?** | A warning letter recommending improved driving skills. |
| S008 | Fully licensed demerit points | **What sanction applies when a fully licensed driver reaches nine demerit points?** | A second warning letter encouraging improved driving behaviour. |
| S009 | Fully licensed demerit points | **What sanction applies when a fully licensed driver reaches 15 demerit points?** | A 30-day licence suspension from the date the licence is surrendered. |
| S010 | Fully licensed demerit points | **After a fully licensed driver's 30-day demerit-point suspension, to how many points is the record reduced?** | Seven points. |
| S011 | Fully licensed demerit points | **What sanction applies if a fully licensed driver reaches 15 demerit points again?** | A six-month licence suspension. |
| S012 | Collision or police | **How many demerit points apply for failing to remain at a collision scene or failing to stop for police?** | Seven demerit points. |
| S013 | Dangerous driving | **How many demerit points apply for careless driving, racing, driving 40 km/h or more over on a road posted below 80 km/h, driving 50 km/h or more over, or failing to stop for a school bus?** | Six demerit points. |
| S014 | Speeding | **How many demerit points apply for driving 30–49 km/h over the speed limit?** | Four demerit points. |
| S015 | Speeding | **How many demerit points apply for driving 16–29 km/h over the speed limit?** | Three demerit points. |
| S016 | Distracted driving | **How many demerit points apply for holding or using a hand-held communications or entertainment device while driving?** | Three demerit points. |
| S017 | Seatbelts | **How many demerit points apply when a driver fails to wear a seatbelt or properly secure a passenger under 16?** | Two demerit points. |
| S018 | Novice restrictions | **What sanction applies when a novice driver violates a graduated-licensing condition?** | A 30-day licence suspension, effective when the licence is surrendered. |
| S019 | Young or novice alcohol | **What immediate sanction applies when a driver age 21 or under violates the zero-BAC requirement?** | An immediate 24-hour roadside licence suspension. |
| S020 | Young or novice alcohol | **What sanctions may follow conviction for violating the zero-BAC requirement?** | A fine and at least a 30-day licence suspension. |
| S021 | Police stop | **What is the minimum licence suspension for failing to stop for a police officer?** | A minimum five-year licence suspension. |
| S022 | Unpaid fine | **What sanction applies if a driver does not pay a traffic fine when ordered by the court?** | The driver's licence is suspended. |
| S023 | BAC over .08 or refusal | **What immediate administrative sanction applies for a BAC over .08 or for refusing a required sample, coordination test, or drug evaluation?** | An immediate 90-day administrative driver's-licence suspension. |
| S024 | Warn range | **What sanction applies for a first occurrence of driving with a BAC from .05 to .08?** | An immediate three-day roadside licence suspension. |
| S025 | Warn range | **What sanctions apply for a second occurrence of driving with a BAC from .05 to .08?** | An immediate seven-day roadside licence suspension plus a remedial alcohol-education program. |
| S026 | Criminal Code | **What licence sanction applies to a first driving-related Criminal Code conviction listed in the handbook?** | A one-year licence suspension. |
| S027 | Criminal Code | **How can licence sanctions escalate after subsequent driving-related Criminal Code convictions?** | They increase substantially and can reach a lifetime driving ban. |
| S028 | Driving suspended | **What sanctions can follow driving while suspended for a Highway Traffic Act offence?** | Fines in the thousands of dollars, up to six months in jail, and six months added to the existing suspension. |
| S029 | Driving prohibited | **What licence sanction applies for violating a Criminal Code driving-prohibition order?** | A one-year suspension for a first offence or a two-year suspension for a subsequent offence. |
| S030 | Vehicle impoundment | **What impoundment applies when driving under an HTA suspension, without a required ignition interlock, with a BAC over .08, or after refusing a police demand?** | A seven-day vehicle impoundment. |
| S031 | Criminal suspension | **What impoundment applies when someone drives while suspended for a Criminal Code offence?** | The vehicle is impounded for at least 45 days. |
| S032 | Impaired driving | **What sanctions can impaired driving bring, depending on prior convictions?** | A fine up to $50,000, jail, or permanent loss of the driver's licence, along with measures such as vehicle impoundment and ignition interlock. |
| S033 | Bodily harm | **What maximum prison sentence can apply to impaired driving causing bodily harm?** | Up to 14 years in prison. |
| S034 | Death | **What maximum prison sentence can apply to impaired driving causing death?** | Imprisonment for life. |
